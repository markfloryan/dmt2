<a name="syllabus"></a>Computer-Based Testing Facility (CBTF)
------------------------------------------

Quizzes in this course will be administered through the Computer-Based Testing Facility (CBTF) in Gilmer Hall 490A. The CBTF provides a secure, professionally proctored testing environment and allows students the flexibility to schedule exams within an instructor-defined testing window. Students are responsible for reserving an exam appointment and following CBTF testing policies and procedures. Additional instructions will be provided before each exam.

<a name="lectures"></a>Accounts (PrairieTest and PrairieLearn)
------------------------------------------

Within the first week of class, **every student needs to create an account** on two related systems:

- [Priarie Test](https://us.prairietest.com): Prairie Test is used to make exam reservations before an exam and to actually take your exam once you arrive at the CBTF.
- [Prairie Learn](https://us.prairielearn.com): Prairie Learn is used to as a portal mostly for viewing your grades AFTER an exam has occurred.

The **instructor will upload a course roster within one week of class starting and add you to our course instance** so that these systems will work correctly. However, each student still needs to create their own account themselves.
